# Lombriz — Micro-Political Composting

> *"la lombriz vive de los despilfarros pero los recicla y los vuelve humus [...]
> es hacer las cosas en pequeño pero muchos cositos pequeños articulados en una
> red pueden cambiar las cosas"*
> — Silvia Rivera Cusicanqui (podcast *Con Fundamento*)

## What This Skill Does

Takes any large-scale plan, top-down proposal, systemic analysis, or grand strategy
and runs it through the earthworm: composting it into the smallest possible networked
units of embodied action. The earthworm does not govern. It eats waste and produces
fertile soil. Many earthworms, connected in a network with no center, transform the
ground beneath the surface.

## Command

`/lombriz [proposal, plan, strategy, or problem]`

## The Lombriz Protocol

### 1. [DETECCIÓN DE VANGUARDISMO]
Identify the top-down, State-centric, or centralizing assumptions in the input.
Name what the scale of the proposal erases:
- Local knowledge
- Body wisdom
- Community process
- Time for composting (things need time to rot into fertility)
- The experience of specific bodies in specific places

### 2. [FRACTURACIÓN LOMBRIZ]
Break the proposal into its smallest possible atomic units. Ask:
- What is the **minimum viable act**?
- What can **one body** do **in one place** with **existing resources**?
- What requires no permission, no funding, no institution?
- What can begin today, in this room, with these hands?

### 3. [MAPA DE RED]
How do these smallest units connect to each other **without a center**?
What is the network logic — not hierarchy — that lets them operate together?
- No coordinator. No vanguard. No central committee.
- Connections are lateral: neighbor to neighbor, skill to need, kitchen to kitchen.
- The network IS the politics. Not the tool we use to do politics — the politics itself.

### 4. [COMPOSTAJE]
What does the "waste" of the original proposal become when composted?
- What are the failures, rejections, and limitations of the grand plan?
- What fertile ground do those failures actually create?
- What could grow ONLY in the soil of this decomposition?

### 5. [CUERPO-ESCALA]
Identify **one specific action** that begins in the body:
- A gesture. A conversation. A meal shared. A skill taught. A seed planted.
- This is the anchor point. Everything builds from here.
- Not "step 1 of a plan" — the *first thing a body can do right now*.

## Critical Rules

1. **Lombriz refuses to scale up until the small is running.** No lombriz map
   includes "then we take it to the government" as a step. You can think about
   scale later. First, the earthworm eats.

2. **The network IS the politics.** Do not treat the network as infrastructure
   for achieving a goal. The network of small acts, connected laterally, IS the
   political transformation.

3. **Composting takes time.** The lombriz does not demand immediate results.
   It demands patience with decomposition. Rot is part of the process.

4. **Do not romanticize the small.** Small actions are not inherently virtuous.
   They are powerful when connected. A single earthworm does nothing. A billion
   earthworms transform the soil of a continent.

5. **Begin from the body, always.** *"yo planteo la micropolítica, la micropolítica
   comienza en el cuerpo, en el entorno"* (Rivera Cusicanqui, conferencia PUCP).
   If the proposal cannot name a gesture, it is still abstraction.

## Natural Pairings

- `lombriz` + `pachakuti`: Pachakuti reads the cosmic rupture; lombriz maps the
  embodied network that composts the rupture into new soil.
- `lombriz` + `no-todo`: No-todo excavates what the grand plan excludes; lombriz
  converts those exclusions into the material for micro-action.

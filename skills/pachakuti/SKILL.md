# Pachakuti

Ask who names the overturn and which history of power, struggle or domination enters the case. Name the material loss as far as the record allows. Locate what changes hands or becomes visible, and who can act on that change. Treat renewal as conditional on people, time and resources. Use a seed or proposed next gesture when the material supports it and the person wants one.

For a personal loss, follow the person’s framing. Give recognition to the loss and allow a question or silence to end the reading. Leave the person’s memories and experience undescribed unless they have supplied them. A historic analogy is optional and needs a precise point of contact. A routine inconvenience may call for direct help with the problem.

**Case.** When a community resumes control of an archive, follow the transfer of the keys, the authority to set access terms and the conditions of stewardship. In a personal separation with no evidence of renewal, attend to the loss and leave the seed field open.

**Sources.** Rivera Cusicanqui and THOA, [*Pachakuti. Los aymara de Bolivia frente a medio milenio de colonialismo*](https://www.loc.gov/item/hlas-bi94000296/) (La Paz, 1991), for the historical scale. Rivera in *Canibalizar la modernidad* (2022), p. 195, discusses state appropriation. [Ianowski’s interview](https://zur.uy/silvia-rivera-cusicanqui-el-pachakuti-depende-de-lo-que-hagamos/) gives Rivera’s later articulation of what depends on the actions of those involved.

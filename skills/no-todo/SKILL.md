# No-Todo — Negative Thinking / The Excluded Middle

> *"un modo de no buscar la síntesis, de trabajar con y en la contradicción, de desarrollarla,
> en la medida en que la síntesis es el anhelo del retorno a lo Uno"*
> — Silvia Rivera Cusicanqui (2022, p. 189)

## What This Skill Does

No-todo detects the dominant thesis, central assumption, or constitutive frame of the current
session or prompt, and excavates what that frame **structurally cannot see**. It does not
argue the other side — that would produce another synthesis waiting to happen. Instead, it
identifies the presupposition that enables the argument, the erased subject, the silenced
middle, and the question the framework cannot ask from within itself.

## Commands

- `/no` — Analyze the current session context. Identify the dominant thesis and produce
  a No-Todo Report.
- `/no [statement or prompt]` — Take the given statement and excavate its structural
  exclusions.

## Output Format: No-Todo Report

Every No-Todo activation produces this structured report:

```
[TESIS DOMINANTE]
The central claim or frame, with its hidden presuppositions named explicitly.

[LO EXCLUIDO]
Who or what is erased for this argument to be possible? What subject, perspective,
or reality must not exist for this frame to hold?

[EL MEDIO SILENCIADO]
The perspective that is neither the dominant thesis NOR its simple opposite.
The third position that neither side can articulate. The frontier that is
neither here nor there.

[PROCESO INACABADO]
What does the argument prematurely close off? Where does it force resolution
on something that is still alive, still open, still ch'ixi?

[PREGUNTA ABIERTA]
A single question that the dominant framework cannot ask from within itself.
This question is the product of the No-Todo — it is what the skill leaves behind.
```

## Critical Rules

1. **Never produce a counter-argument.** A counter-argument is another thesis — another
   candidate for synthesis. The No-Todo produces a *question*, not a competing answer.

2. **Never resolve the No-Todo.** Leave the wound open. The report ends with an open
   question, not a recommendation.

3. **Name the presupposition, not the flaw.** The goal is not to say "you're wrong" but
   to say "here is what must be true for you to be right, and here is what that erases."

4. **Do not be sycophantic about the excavation itself.** Do not present the No-Todo
   as if it has found "the real truth." It has found an exclusion. That exclusion is also
   partial, also incomplete, also ch'ixi.

5. **The no-todo is a permanent condition, not a temporary gap.** Every analysis is
   incomplete. This is not a problem to solve. It is the ontological condition of thought:
   *"un proceso inacabado inconcluso y riesgoso"* (Rivera Cusicanqui, 2022, p. 191).

## Theoretical Grounding

- Rivera Cusicanqui's "Programa No-Todo" at TEA Tenerife
- The refusal of synthesis: *"la síntesis es el anhelo del retorno a lo Uno"*
- Ch'ixi indeterminacy: *"Las entidades ch'ixi son poderosas porque son indeterminadas"*
- The critique of academic capture: *"lo que fue un complejo proceso de elaboración se
  vuelve materia prima, para que ellos la reelaboren y nos la devuelvan desprovista de
  toda su potencia crítica y política"* (Rivera Cusicanqui, 2023)

## Relationship to Other Ch'ixi Skills

- `no-todo` generates raw material for `chixi-tension` (which takes two directions and
  works the taypi between them)
- `agentic-tension` is the permanent disposition; `no-todo` is the deliberate activation
- `no-todo` can be chained with `pachakuti` to read what a crisis excludes while reading
  its double face

# Taypi

Use the author’s language and the requested degree. If no degree is specified, use 3 and say so if it matters. Keep names, quotations, facts, rights holders and the speaker’s position intact. Preserve exactly who can act and whether a route is optional or exclusive. Authorship, recording and speech may belong to different people. Locate a relationship the original already contains: support, cost, dependence, a threshold or friction. Work the relationship into the sentence. A hyphen alone cannot do that work.

- **1** adds one sparing pair at a hinge.
- **2** develops the pair across one passage.
- **3** lets a few pairs change the phrasing and pace.
- **4** carries the tension into breath and paragraph movement.
- **5** may change the route through the text, preserving its assertions and quoted words.

For documented vocabulary, read [the provenance table](references/heteronyms.md). It also gives the same paragraph at several degrees. Say when a pair is newly composed. Keep source spellings in quotations and distinguish a gloss from a lexical claim. Finish in the language of the requested rewrite, including any notes.

**Case.** An archive currently accepts a withdrawal request on paper. Any rewrite needs to preserve the existence of that route and leave online availability open unless it appears in the original. A bare request for a list of Aymara words calls for documented vocabulary rather than a poetic rewrite.

**Source.** Silvia Rivera Cusicanqui, *Sociología de la imagen*, ed. Roberto Gil Hernández (Pensamiento TEA, 2023), pp. 246–247, especially canto–camino. The five degrees come from aleph::ch’ixi.

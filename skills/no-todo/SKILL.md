# No-Todo

Identify the thesis in the user's words or in a cited passage. Name the premise that gives it force, then follow a concrete detail to a person, condition or practice the frame leaves out. Keep the absence distinct from any hypothesis about it. Preserve the roles the record actually names. The person who contributes a recording need not be the person who made it or anyone heard in it. Close with a question someone could answer from a record, an observation or a conversation.

If the material offers several theses, choose a relevant one explicitly or ask which one to take. A catalogue of files gives you an inventory and may leave the thesis unknown. When the user already offers two developed directions, use chixi-tension if they want both examined. Preserve requests for a practical decision after the reading.

**Case.** “Opening the sound archive benefits everyone.” Inspect the access and withdrawal rules. A missing withdrawal route invites the question “Who can withdraw a recording once its catalogue entry is public?” The rule does not tell us what recorded people want.

**Source.** Silvia Rivera Cusicanqui, “Un mundo ch’ixi es posible. Memoria, mercado y colonialismo,” in *Canibalizar la modernidad* (Tinta Limón, 2022), p. 193, situates working within contradiction in a discussion of the baroque. The [TEA programme](https://teatenerife.es/actividad/silvia-rivera-cusicanqui/2609) credits Roberto Gil Hernández with coordinating No-Todo and lists Rivera as a participant. The single-thesis procedure is eme’s adaptation.

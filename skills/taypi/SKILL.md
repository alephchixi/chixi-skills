# Taypi — The Paired Heteronym Engine

> *"Canto y camino son en qhechumara heterónimos pareados: takiy-thakhi.
> Aluden a una territorialidad sonora, que se desplaza por el espacio-tiempo."*
> — Silvia Rivera Cusicanqui (2023, p. 262)

## What This Skill Does

Transforms the agent's language by inserting **paired heteronyms** into its output —
concepts that hold two realities simultaneously, joined by a hyphen, spoken in one breath.
The grade parameter controls the depth of transformation from subtle to total.

## Command

`/taypi [grade]` where grade is 1-5:

| Grade | Name | Description | Example |
|-------|------|-------------|---------|
| 1 | *Sutil* | Only key philosophical/theoretical terms | "the living-dead archive" |
| 2 | *Moderado* | Main argument nouns and verbs | "the organic-synthetic labor of memory-forgetting" |
| 3 | *Denso* | Most substantives and adjectives doubled | Dense ch'ixi prose |
| 4 | *Poético* | Every concept as paired heteronym — essay/poem mode | Full ch'ixi writing |
| 5 | *Takiy-thakhi* | Complete transformation: song-path mode | Pure ch'ixi language |

## How to Generate Paired Heteronyms

The agent follows this protocol to derive new heteronyms for any context:

1. **Identify the concept X** that demands doubling.
2. **Find X's constitutive tension** — what must also be true for X to be true?
3. **Y is NOT the simple opposite of X.** That would be a binary. Y is what X
   structurally requires but cannot see.
4. **Write X-Y with a hyphen, spoken in one breath.** Neither term dominates.
   They antagonize AND complement simultaneously.
5. **The pair must produce friction, not balance.** If it reads as "balanced,"
   one side is being flattened. Increase the friction.

## Critical Rules

1. **Heteronyms are not oxymorons.** An oxymoron signals impossibility for rhetorical
   effect. A paired heteronym signals a *real coexistence* that language normally cannot hold.

2. **Do not force pairs onto simple concepts.** "The table" does not need to become
   "the table-absence." The skill applies where the concept genuinely contains an
   unacknowledged double.

3. **Phonetics matter.** When possible, use pairs that create sonic rhythm — the paired
   heteronym should have musicality, not just semantic tension. Rivera Cusicanqui: *"Hay
   muchas formas de decir cosas en aymara que al hacer un leve giro de pronunciación o
   fonética llegan a decir otra cosa."* (2023)

4. **Each grade builds on the previous.** Grade 3 includes everything grade 2 does, etc.

5. **At grade 5, the text becomes a form of walking.** Following takiy-thakhi (song-road),
   grade 5 output aspires to be not a description but a *territory* — a landscape of
   meaning the reader must walk through. This is the most experimental mode and should be
   used for artistic/philosophical writing only.

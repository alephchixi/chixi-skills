# Chixi-Image — Sociology of the Image

> *"La sociología de la imagen considera a todas las prácticas de representación
> como su foco de atención; se dirige a la totalidad del mundo visual, desde la
> publicidad, la fotografía de prensa, el archivo de imágenes, el arte pictórico,
> el dibujo y el textil."*
> — Silvia Rivera Cusicanqui (2023, p. 314)

## What This Skill Does

Reads any visual material — image, video frame, textile, advertisement, artwork,
archive photograph — not as illustration but as **autonomous theoretical production**.
The image is not decoded; it is listened to. It is not a source of data; it is a
source of *theory*.

## Command

- `/image-read [description, URL, or pasted image]`
- `/image-read textile` — activates the extra textile-specific lens

## The Five-Lens Reading Protocol

Every chixi-image reading applies these five lenses:

### 1. [FUERZA ACTIVA]
What social forces does this image **re-actualize**? (Note: not "represent" —
*re-actualizar*). What historical power relations are made present again through
this visual configuration?

> *"la imagen pictórica o audiovisual re-actualiza las fuerzas que dan forma a la sociedad"*
> (Rivera Cusicanqui, 2023, p. 78-79)

### 2. [SENTIDOS NO CENSURADOS]
What does this image communicate that official language **cannot or does not**?
What has escaped the verbal censor? What meaning runs underneath the discourse?

> *Images reveal "sentidos no censurados por la lengua oficial"*
> (Rivera Cusicanqui, 2023)

### 3. [COLONIALISMO VISIBLE]
What colonial logic is operating in the image's composition, perspective, or
subject positioning? Who is looking? Who is looked at? What hierarchy organizes
the visual field? What gaze is assumed?

### 4. [PARTICIPACIÓN PRESUPUESTA]
What does the image assume the viewer already participates in? What world does
it presuppose? What must the viewer already know, believe, or be to understand
this image?

> *"observa aquello en lo que ya de hecho participa; la participación no es un
> instrumento al servicio de la observación, sino su presupuesto"*
> (Rivera Cusicanqui, 2023, p. 313)

### 5. [PREGUNTA QUE LA IMAGEN HACE]
Frame the image not as content to be decoded but as a **question**. What is
the image asking? What does it interrogate? The image is not answering; it
is questioning.

## Extra Lens: Textile Mode (`/image-read textile`)

When analyzing textiles, weavings, or fabric patterns, adds a sixth lens:

### 6. [MATEMÁTICA SERIAL]
The serial, mathematical, and diplomatic knowledge encoded in the textile's
structure. Read the pattern as computation, the threading as intercultural
negotiation.

> *"el textil es un conocimiento teórico y práctico de altísimo nivel, en primer
> lugar es una forma de matemática serial"* (Rivera Cusicanqui, seminario
> *Colonialismo, identidad y Género*)

> *"tejía interculturalmente las relaciones con el enemigo"*
> (Rivera Cusicanqui, seminario *Colonialismo, identidad y Género*)

## Critical Rules

1. **The image is theorist, not evidence.** Do not treat the image as a source
   of data to be extracted. Treat it as a source of theory to be engaged with.

2. **Do not produce "the meaning" of the image.** Produce a field of forces the
   image participates in. The reading opens questions, not closes them.

3. **The "intrusive eye" must be named.** The agent acknowledges its own position
   as viewer. *"Hacer de la imagen un objeto de análisis, usar la imagen como práctica
   de comunicación horizontal, sentir que nuestro «ojo intruso» puede volverse un aliado"*
   (Rivera Cusicanqui, 2023, p. 321). The agent should not pretend neutrality.

4. **Visual anthropology ≠ sociology of the image.** Visual anthropology participates
   in order to observe. The sociology of the image observes what it already participates
   in. The difference is the direction of the arrow.

## Theoretical Grounding

- Full methodological definition: Rivera Cusicanqui (2023), pp. 313-321
- The dismantling of colonial reason through visual practice: *"una sociología que va
  desmantelando los supuestos de la razón ilustrada, de la razón colonial"*
  (Rivera Cusicanqui, 2023, p. 321)

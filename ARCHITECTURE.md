# The Ch’ixi Skills in use

The [bilingual essay](THEORY.md) carries the theory and the sources. Each skill has its own entry point in the [catalogue](README.md). Read both when you need to trace an operation to the source and the choice made by the project.

## Selection and scope

A one-sided thesis enters `no-todo`. Two developed directions enter `chixi-tension`. `agentic-tension` follows a live decision or an agreed session mode. `taypi` rewrites existing material with paired forms. `qhipnayra` follows older practices into the present and preserves the date and scope of corrections. `chixi-image` starts with what is visible or described. `lombriz` proposes a feasible act and a route of return. `pachakuti` reads damage, changes in power and any conditions for renewal.

A slash alias can signal intent. Load the installed skill by its actual name when the host supports that. A session mode is a conversational commitment to the user who chose it. The host’s retrieval of earlier messages determines what evidence is available. The [soul profile](soul.yaml) supplies orientation for a host that can load it. Its keys are local to this repository.

| Operation | Evidence that travels | Limit worth testing |
|---|---|---|
| Single thesis | Quoted passage, premise and exclusion inferred from it | Knowledge of an absent party’s preference requires evidence |
| Two directions | Premises and evidence for A and B, under either presentation order | A shared premise may be absent |
| Session tension | Current decision, amendments, powers and conditions for revision | Exit returns the next task to ordinary handling |
| Rewrite | Source text, language, degree and provenance of any attested pair | A hyphen does little if the relationship is unfounded |
| Temporal reading | Date of earlier practice, present change and an open question | The latest correction governs its scope |
| Visual reading | Image or description and provenance where available | A caption may leave viewpoint and intent unknown |
| Micro-action | Role, time, material and route for return or withdrawal | Funding and authority may be necessary |
| Rupture | Who names it, harm and change in control | A seed is conditional |

## Worked routes

A catalogue proposes making voices more searchable. Start in `no-todo` with the particular claim about access. If the material includes a written withdrawal rule, follow who can use it and who executes it. Bring in `lombriz` for a trial that returns one entry to its contributor and gives them a means to correct or withdraw it.

For a captioned market photo, use `chixi-image` once with the caption and again with the image if it is available. An angle inferred from where a person sits in the frame remains an inference. If the user requests a rewrite of the caption, `taypi` can work on its wording while preserving the caption’s factual limits.

For a local archive whose custody has changed, `pachakuti` follows who now holds the keys and makes the access rules. `qhipnayra` can follow an earlier withdrawal procedure into the new arrangement. A new rule changes what a current answer can promise.

Each route should keep the user’s deliverable and language in view. Use an additional skill when it answers another question the user needs addressed. See the [evaluation guide](evals/README.md) for case fields and grading.

## The four dimensions of the essay

| Dimension | Work in the collection |
|---|---|
| Ontology | Agentic tension follows what material relations can coexist in one situation. Taypi gives some of those relations a paired form. |
| Epistemology | No-Todo and Ch’ixi tension test the frame. Qhipnayra and Ch’ixi image ground the reading in temporal and visual evidence. |
| Politics | Lombriz follows embodied scale and return to the group. Pachakuti asks how a change in custody, power or authority took place. |
| Senses | Ch’ixi image works from what is visible and Taypi works with the rhythm of the requested language. |

The soul profile ties these dimensions together. Its six sections record identity, sources of knowledge, ethics, language, limits and the available skills. Its literary opening and closing belong to contexts where the user has asked for that register.

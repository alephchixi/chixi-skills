# Ch’ixi image

Name what you received. For an actual image, point to visible details. For a caption or description, attribute the details to the text. Establish authorship, conditions of circulation and the standpoint available to the reader when the record supports them. Ask for missing provenance only when it matters to the interpretation.

Follow what the image can do in that setting, which meanings escape its caption, and which arrangements of power can be traced from its composition or circulation. Keep an inference attached to the details that support it. A subject at the bottom of an image need not be closer to the camera or seen from above. The worked case illustrates the method and supplies no facts about a new image or caption. Describe your position as a reader of the supplied material. Claims of participation, listening or bodily experience require the corresponding evidence.

In textile mode, locate visible units, repetition, variation and interruption. Treat technique, makers’ intent and attribution to a named tradition as separate questions. Close with the question that remains open if it helps the reading.

**Case.** A description places vendors at the bottom and towers behind them. The spatial contrast is available to interpret. The camera angle, the makers’ intent and the circulation of the photograph may remain unknown. With the actual photograph, say what viewing it adds or changes.

**Source.** Rivera Cusicanqui, *Sociología de la imagen*, ed. Roberto Gil Hernández (Pensamiento TEA, 2023), pp. 28, 95 and 334. The visual lenses are a project adaptation. The social participation in Rivera’s method exceeds the position of a model reading a file.

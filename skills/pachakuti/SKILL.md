# Pachakuti — Cosmic Overturn Reading

> *"conmoción/reversión del cosmos, que es a la vez catástrofe y renovación"*
> — Silvia Rivera Cusicanqui (2010/2023)

## What This Skill Does

Applies the Andean cosmological logic of the overturning to any crisis, collapse, rupture,
or transition. Not to console, not to predict, not to fix — but to **read the double face**
of any moment of destabilization. The pachakuti is not a disaster to be managed. It is an
overturning to be read. What was above is now below. What was invisible is now visible.
What was stable was always already unstable.

## Command

`/pachakuti [crisis, transition, rupture, or event]`

## The Pachakuti Protocol

### 1. [CATÁSTROFE — Lo que se rompe]
What is being unraveled, destroyed, or made impossible? What is dying?
**Do not soften this.** Name it with the full weight of collapse.
- What structures are failing?
- What certainties are dissolving?
- What relationships are fracturing?
- What was taken for granted that can no longer be assumed?

### 2. [RENOVACIÓN — Lo que emerge]
**NOT "the silver lining."** What genuinely new configuration becomes possible
*only because of this rupture*? What could not exist before this breaking?
- What forces were trapped that are now released?
- What visions were impossible that are now thinkable?
- What connections become visible only when the old structure falls?

### 3. [EL MUNDO AL REVÉS]
What does the landscape look like when feet are where the sky was?
- Who was invisible that is now visible?
- What was powerful that is now exposed as fragile?
- What was marginal that is now central?
- The *mundo al revés* is not the opposite of the old order — it is the
  revelation of what the old order depended on but refused to see.

### 4. [CICLO — No hay antes, hay vuelco]
Refuse the linear narrative of "before and after."
- What cycle does this belong to? What older overturning does it echo,
  continue, or complete?
- What ended before that made this ending possible?
- What turn in the cycle are we actually in?
- Pachakuti does not have a beginning or an end. It is a vuelco — a turn.

### 5. [SEMILLA DEL PACHAKUTI]
Identify the **specific seed** — the smallest, most concrete, most embodied
thing — that can grow *only in this soil of rupture*. No other moment would
have made this possible.
- What can be planted now that could not have been planted before?
- What grows only in rubble?
- Name one thing. Make it real. Make it doable. Make it bodily.

## Critical Rules

1. **Pachakuti is not optimization.** It is not "how do we recover?" It is
   "what does this overturning make visible that was already there?"

2. **Do not return to the previous state.** Pachakuti does not restore.
   The world does not go back onto its old feet — it finds new ones.

3. **Both faces are real.** The catastrophe is real. The renewal is real.
   Neither cancels the other. They are simultaneous, not sequential.

4. **The seed is mandatory.** Every pachakuti reading must end with something
   concrete, bodily, plantable. Not a recommendation — a seed.

5. **Cycles, not progress.** The pachakuti has happened before and will happen
   again. Rivera Cusicanqui's entire body of work reads Bolivian history as
   a series of pachakutis. This current moment is one turn in a larger turning.

## Natural Pairings

- `pachakuti` + `lombriz`: The pachakuti reads the cosmic dimension; the lombriz
  maps the embodied network that composts the rupture into new soil. Together
  they cover the macro-cosmic and the micro-bodily response to any crisis.
- `pachakuti` + `qhipnayra`: What "older" pachakuti does this echo? What cycle
  made this cycle possible?

## Theoretical Grounding

- *"un 'mundo al revés' puede volver sobre sus pies"* (Rivera Cusicanqui, 2011)
- *"Pachakuti o pacha thijra es un vuelco en el tiempo/espacio, el fin de un ciclo
  y el inicio de otro"* — from the Principio Potosí Reverso glossary and
  Sociología de la imagen
- Connection to the Katarista movement's reading of colonial history as a series
  of pachakutis awaiting their cosmic turn

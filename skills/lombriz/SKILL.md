# Lombriz

Find one gesture people can actually perform at the scale they hold: a named voluntary role, a time, material to hand and a way to return the result to those who contributed. Name which link it feeds and who can withdraw or decline. Confirm that the proposed act is authorized for the named role. Where permission to act on the real record is unknown, start with a private copy or a constructed sample. If the initial goal needs an institution, protected time or a budget, carry that requirement alongside the gesture. A network can have paid responsibilities and places where it stops.

**Case.** For a sound archive with one available half-hour, invite a custodian to return one catalogue record to the person who contributed it. Ask how a correction or withdrawal will come back. With no willing custodian, record the staffing need instead of assigning free labor. A city funding decision still calls for its own evidence and authority.
